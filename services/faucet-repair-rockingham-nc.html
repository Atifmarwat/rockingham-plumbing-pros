Common Faucet Issues That May Need Repair

Faucets are used regularly and can cause internal components to wear. Some issues will develop over time.

Dripping/Leaking Faucets

A dripping faucet can be caused by a worn cartridge, washer, valve seat or seal depending on the design of your faucet. Turning the handle too tightly will not always be a viable solution and could cause damage. Leaky faucet repair may involve replacing the faulty internal component.

Leakage can also occur from your spout, handle or base. Knowing where the water is coming from can help identify what the cause of the issue may be. An inspection may be required to identify what is causing the fault.

Poor Water Pressure

Low water pressure may be caused by a blocked aerator, limescale, a blocked hose or an internal issue. If other fixtures throughout your property are having the same issues it may be due to an issue with the plumbing.

By comparing the faulty faucet to others in the area this can give useful information.

Loose Handles

Handles that are too loose, stiff or require extra effort may be caused by a loose fixing, worn internal components or build up. If your handle is no longer controlling the temperature or flow of water it could be due to a faulty cartridge or valve.

If you try to force your handle it can cause more damage. By having a proper assessment it can help determine the best course of action and whether adjustments, cleaning or replacements are needed.

Leaking From Base Of Faucet Or Under Sink

Water leaking from the base of your faucet could be due to a faulty seal, loose fittings or water leaking from the body. Water could also be leaking from your sink cabinet due to faulty connections, shut off valve or drain.

It is important to identify what the actual cause of the issue is. Water can sometimes appear to be leaking from the faucet but actually be due to a connection in the plumbing.

What Causes Faucet Issues?

Depending on the design of your faucet there are a variety of internal components. Knowing the potential causes can help understand why we start by inspecting.

Worn Washers/O-Rings/Seal

Rubber washers and O-rings help to prevent water leaking from moving components. Over time these can wear due to regular use, age and deterioration.

If your seal is worn it can cause dripping, leaking from the handle or dampness around the base. Replacing the correct seal can help fix this issue if the surrounding components are in good condition.

Faulty Cartridge

Many modern faucets contain cartridges to control the flow and temperature of the water. A faulty or worn cartridge can cause dripping, difficult to turn handles and inconsistent temperature.

Depending on the design some faucets contain compression stems, ceramic discs or ball. The repair will depend on what design, make and replacement components are available. A compatible faucet repair kit may contain components required for certain repairs.

Limescale Build Up & Blocked Aerator

Limescale can build up inside the openings of your faucet and aerator. This can cause low water flow and an inconsistent flow of water.

If the issue is due to a blocked aerator then cleaning or replacing can help return water flow to normal. If there is a large amount of build up inside the faucet then you may need further attention.

Corrosion, Loose Connections & Water Supply Issues

Corrosion can effect metal components and connections. If your supply connections are loose you may have water leaking underneath your sink. Faulty hoses and shut off valves can also cause leaks.

A technician will want to know if the issue is due to a faulty fixture or water supply.

Types Of Faucets

Depending on the type of faucet you have will impact what parts, tools and repairs are needed.

Kitchen Faucet

Kitchen faucets can have pull down sprays, pull out hoses, side sprays, swivel spouts and single handle. Common issues include leaking spray heads, faulty hoses, loose handles, dripping spout and low water flow. Kitchen faucet repair may be needed when these components become faulty.

Some faucets will also contain extra connections or diverters. Knowing what model and make you have can help identify what replacement parts are compatible.

Bathroom Sink Faucet

There are single handle, widespread, centreset and wall mounted faucets. Common causes for repair include dripping, loose handles, leaking base and blocked aerator. Bathroom faucet repair can address these types of issues.

The space underneath your sink and how your faucet is fitted can impact how easy it is to inspect and repair.

Bathroom & Shower Faucet

These can contain cartridges, pressure balancing valves or other internal components. Common issues include dripping from the bath spout, difficult to control temperature and dripping after turning the handle off. Shower faucet repair may be needed if the internal components are faulty.

If you have a leak behind your shower wall this is different to a faulty connection. If there are hidden pipework then further investigation may be needed.

Single Handle & Double Handle Faucet

Single handle faucets usually have one control to control the flow and temperature. Double handle faucets have separate hot and cold controls. Due to the difference in internal components the repair should be carried out according to the design.

Professional Faucet Repairs

Depending on the fixture, location of the leak and condition of the components the correct repair will be carried out. Professional faucet repair services can help identify the issue and determine which components need attention.

Replace Washer/O-Ring/Cartridge/Seal

A repair may be needed to replace the washer, O-ring, cartridge or seal. This will help to fix the source of the leak and replace only faulty components.

Ensure the correct size and compatibility with your faucet model. Some models, including certain Delta faucets, may require a compatible delta faucet repair kit.

Adjust Handle, Cartridge & Valve

If your handle is loose then adjustments or replacement may be needed. If your controls are stiff or faulty then the cartridge or valve may need repairing. If the body of your faucet is cracked or heavily corroded then a simple repair may not be enough.

Low/Uneven Water Flow

If your faucet is having issues with low/uneven water flow then the aerator and any accessible water ways may need inspecting. This could be due to a blocked hose.

If multiple fixtures are having similar issues then it could be due to an issue with the whole plumbing system.

Water Supply Connections

Flexible hoses, shut off valves and threaded connections can become faulty and loose. A proper assessment will include looking at any connections in the area. If there is an issue with water underneath your sink then it may be due to a faulty supply component.

It may be more appropriate to replace a faulty component.

What To Expect During Your Repair

Generally a professional repair will start by discussing your issues. You can tell them when the issue started, if it is constant or intermittent and if other fixtures are having issues.

They may then:

  • Identify which faucet is having issues and look at the visible condition.
  • Look at the handle, spout, aerator and any accessible connections.
  • Identify if it looks like an issue with the fixture, water supply or other plumbing.
  • Tell you what the likely repair will be and any parts that may be needed.
  • Turn off the correct water supply and open up the fixture.
  • Carry out the agreed repair. Check the water flow, temperature controls and visible connections.

Depending on the type of faucet and issues you are having the repair will vary. If your faucet is damaged beyond repair or there are no compatible parts then it may be discussed to replace.

Cost Of Faucet Repairs

Depending on the actual issue and work required to repair your faucet the cost will vary. If your issue is a small aerator or seal issue then this will be different to a faulty cartridge, corroded body or leaky water supply.

Things that may affect the cost include:

  • Design of your faucet – Some faucets have more complex internal components or components that are not as easily accessible.
  • Price of replacement parts – Price and availability of compatible cartridges, valves, seals and hoses.
  • Condition of your fixture – Corrosion, damaged threading or previous repair attempts.
  • Accessibility – If there is a tight space inside your cabinet or hidden pipework.
  • Extra Plumbing Issues – Faulty shut off valve or water supply.
  • Overall condition – If your faucet is in good condition it may be more viable to repair.

Always ask for an explanation of the work that will be carried out. If there are other issues found during the inspection ask if this affects what repair should be carried out and the cost.

When To Book A Professional Faucet Repair

Some issues with your faucet may be small however others can cause ongoing wastage of water and damage to surrounding cabinets. Consider booking an assessment if:

  • Your faucet is dripping after turning it off.
  • Water is appearing around the base of your faucet or underneath your sink.
  • Your handle is loose, stuck or difficult to turn.
  • Your water pressure is changing unexpectedly.
  • Hot and cold controls are not working properly.
  • Previous repairs have not resolved the issue.
  • You can see signs of corrosion, faulty connections or constant leaking.

If there is active leaking turn off the water supply using the accessible shut off valve. If you cannot control the leak or it appears to be due to hidden pipework do not attempt to dismantle the faucet and contact the right plumbing service.

How To Prepare For Your Repair

Having a little preparation can help to identify the issue. Ensure you clear any items underneath the affected sink to allow the technician to access the shut off valves and water supply.

It may also be useful to note:

  • Is the leak constant or only when in use?
  • Where is the leak coming from? Spout, handle, base or cabinet?
  • Is it affecting the hot, cold or both?
  • Are other faucets having similar pressure issues?
  • What brand/model is your faucet?
  • Have you had any repairs attempted?

If there is water inside your cabinet remove anything stored and dry any areas you can safely. Do not use any electrical equipment.

FAQ

Can a dripping faucet be repaired?

Often yes. A dripping faucet may just need a compatible washer, O-ring, cartridge or valve. Depending on the design of your faucet and condition of internal components the correct repair can be carried out.

Why is my faucet leaking when I turn the handle off?

A worn cartridge, faulty seal, washer or valve component may be causing your faucet not to fully shut. This will depend on the mechanism of your fixture and should be checked before replacement.

Why is my kitchen faucet losing water pressure?

A blocked aerator, limescale, blocked hose or water supply issue may be causing your water pressure to drop. If other fixtures are having the same issue it may be due to an issue with your property plumbing.

Should I repair or replace my old faucet?

If your faucet is in good structural condition and there are compatible parts available then consider repairing. If your faucet is heavily corroded, cracked, constantly breaking or difficult to repair then it may be more practical to replace.

Can a leaking faucet cause damage underneath my sink?

Yes. If there is constant leaking it can cause dampness to your cabinets, stored items and surrounding areas. Check if there is damp underneath your sink and address the source of the issue.

Why is my faucet making noise?

Noise can be caused by blocked water flow, loose components, pressure changes or worn internal components. Knowing what noise it is making and when can help identify the cause.

Can a stuck handle on my bathroom faucet be repaired?

In some cases yes. Mineral build up, corrosion or faulty internal components may be causing your handle to be difficult to move. Do not force your handle as this can cause damage to the handle or valve.

Should I contact a plumber if there is a leak underneath my sink?

It is a good idea to identify the source of the leak. This could be due to a faulty connection, faulty hose, faulty shut off valve or drain. Each will require a different solution.
