When would you need a sewer line replaced?

Your sewer line runs wastewater from your homes plumbing system to a public sewer connection or private sewage system. As most of this piping is underground it can be prone to wearing away without you knowing. If you are experiencing regular issues you can get an inspection arranged.

Signs You May Need Your Sewer Line Replaced

One blocked drain doesn't necessarily mean you need your main sewer line replaced however if you are experiencing issues with multiple drains or recurring issues after cleaning then you may have an issue with your main sewer line.

Signs you may need your sewer line replaced:

  • Multiple drains are running slowly or backing up
  • Constant blockages
  • Bad sewer smells
  • Gurgling from plumbing
  • Wet or soft earth
  • Sewage backing up

These issues could be caused by many things so getting an inspection will help find out if it's a blockage, partial damage or full replacement required.

Common Issues With Damaged Sewer Lines

Sewer pipes can become worn or damaged for various reasons. Over time older pipes can start to corrode, crack and the joints can fail. Tree roots can also become trapped within the pipe through small openings and grow causing a blockage.

Other issues could include:

  • Earth shifting causing pressure on the underground pipes
  • Collapsed or crushed sections
  • Corrosion
  • Loose or broken joints
  • Blocked due to grease, foreign objects and build up
  • Incorrect gradient or incorrect fitting

It's important to know what the issue is as clearing a blockage won't help if your pipe has collapsed. If there isn't a full replacement required then you may not need to replace the pipe.

Sewer Line Inspection

Before replacing your sewer line it is important to understand what the issue is and where it is. Getting a sewer line inspection in Rockingham, NC can help identify any hidden issues.

Camera Inspection

A camera inspection usually involves inserting a special waterproof camera into an accessible cleanout. This allows the technician to see inside the pipe and identify any visible damage or blockage.

Depending on how accessible the pipe is and what equipment is available it can help to identify:

  • Cracks, breaks and loose joints
  • Roots and build up
  • Bent or collapsed pipe
  • Water pooling
  • Pipe material looking worn

A pipe locator may be used to give an indication of where the camera is underground. This can help to identify where excavation may be required. However camera inspections can have limitations with regards to viewing the pipe due to water, blockages and bends.

What to Look For

Based on the inspection you should be able to determine if a partial repair is required or a full replacement. If there is a small section of damage then a partial repair could be suitable. If there is a lot of wear or multiple failed sections then a full replacement may be a more practical option.

As a property owner you should ask for an explanation of what was found. Ask them to explain what they found with the pipe, where the damage is and how much damage there is. Ask them what they are going to do and if there are any alternative options. If it's not clear then ask what further inspections or access may be required.

Sewer Line Repairs or Replacement

Depending on the material, age, condition, depth, accessibility and level of damage the right option will be chosen between a repair or replacement.

Partial Repairs

If during the inspection there is one section of damage and the rest of the pipe is structurally sound then a repair may be suitable. Examples could include small cracks, damaged joints and a small section of root damage. If you are searching for sewer pipe repair near me, an inspection can help determine whether a partial repair is suitable.

This can help reduce any excavation however it won't help with any wear elsewhere. If the rest of the pipe is failing then it may only be a temporary fix.

Full Replacement

If there is a lot of cracking, constant collapsing, significant corrosion, multiple damaged joints or multiple sections failing then a full or extended replacement may be considered. If the existing pipe isn't reliable at transporting wastewater after cleaning then a replacement may be recommended.

Based on the inspection you should decide how much of the pipe is being replaced. Don't assume it's an old pipe and ask if they are replacing the whole private sewer lateral or just a certain section. Also ask where your responsibility ends and the public sewer begins as this can depend on the utility rules and how your property is set up.

Sewer Line Replacement Options

Depending on the existing pipe, route, access points, depth and surrounding structures the right replacement will be chosen. Not all properties will be suitable for all options.

Traditional Replacement

Traditional replacement involves excavating to expose the damaged pipe and replacing it. This may be required if the existing pipe has collapsed significantly, the route needs adjusting or if a trenchless option isn't suitable.

This can involve:

  • Identify the route and section of pipe to replace
  • Plan how to access and excavate
  • Remove the damaged pipe
  • Prepare the trench and install suitable replacement pipe
  • Connect the new section
  • Test the line to ensure it is flowing correctly
  • Backfill the area as agreed

Excavation can cause issues with your landscaping, drive, pathways etc. Before starting ask what will be done to restore these areas.

Trenchless Options

Some sewer line projects can be completed using trenchless options which can reduce the amount of digging required. Common options include pipe bursting and cured in place pipe lining.

Pipe Bursting – Using equipment to break up or push the existing pipe aside whilst pulling a new pipe through. This can depend on the existing pipe, surrounding utilities, soil type, access and space.

Pipe Lining – A liner is inserted into the existing pipe and cured. Often this is referred to as rehabilitating as it isn't a full replacement. This may not be suitable if the pipe has collapsed significantly, certain bends or if there are any structural issues.

Ask the professional what option they are going to use and why it is suitable for your pipe. Don't promise a trenchless option until the line has been inspected.

Sewer Line Installation

Installing a sewer line is different to replacing a damaged pipe. This could be required if you are building new, adding onto a building, moving the connection or require a new route.

Generally the route will be planned, the pipe size and gradient will be identified, the connection will be organised and any relevant plumbing and local requirements will be met. Ensuring the gradient is correct is important as wastewater lines need to flow correctly. If the gradient isn't correct, connection is poor or the wrong materials are used issues can occur with drainage.

In a replacement scenario the new pipe may run along the same route however due to site conditions and the condition of surrounding utilities changes may be required. Ask if any permits, inspections, utility organisation and reinstatement is included.

Cost Considerations

There isn't a reliable cost for every sewer line replacement. The Sewer line replacement rockingham nc cost will depend on the actual condition of the pipe.

Key considerations could include:

  • Length – The longer the length of pipe the more materials and labour will be required.
  • Depth – If the pipe is deep or there isn't much room to work it can be more complicated.
  • Material – The replacement pipe will need to be suitable for your system.
  • Damage – If there are collapsed sections, roots, groundwater and difficult connections it can impact the work.
  • Replacement – Traditional and trenchless options will have different equipment.
  • Permits – Permits may be required.
  • Reinstatement – Restoring any landscaping, concrete, paving etc can impact the final cost.

To get a reliable cost ask for a written quotation. This should include what section of pipe, what option is being used, materials, testing, permits and reinstatement. Ask what could impact the cost once the area is open.

What to Do If Your Sewer Line Fails

If you're experiencing sewage backing up into your property try to avoid using any water. This will add more wastewater to your system. Keep people and pets away from any contaminated areas. Avoid touching any sewage. Don't attempt to go into any sewer openings or dig around any underground utility lines.

If you're having issues with multiple fixtures, sewage is escaping outside or the issue is still present once basic drain use has stopped then get a professional to assess. If the company advertise emergency services ask what their actual availability is.

FAQ

How do I know if I need my sewer line replaced?

If you are experiencing recurring backflow, multiple slow drains, constant bad smells, wet ground or constant blockages you may have an issue with your sewer line. A suitable inspection can help determine if it's a blockage, partial damage or significant damage to the pipe.

Is it better to replace rather than repair?

Not always. If there is one section of damage and the rest of the pipe is fine then a repair may be suitable. If there is a lot of damage, collapsed pipe or multiple sections failing then a replacement may be a better option. This should be decided after assessing the line.

Will I need a camera inspection?

Often a camera inspection is useful as it can show you the condition of an accessible sewer pipe. It can help identify any damage and pinpoint where the damage is. If the camera can't go through the pipe then another inspection may be required.

Can all sewer lines be replaced using trenchless methods?

Not always. It can depend on the condition of the pipe, route, material, access and surrounding site conditions. If the pipe has significantly collapsed or isn't suitable then excavation may be required. Once the pipe has been assessed explain what options are available.

Will clearing my sewer drain fix my broken pipe?

Clearing can help with certain blockages however it won't fix any structural issues. If your pipe is cracked, collapsed or loose then clearing won't help. An inspection can help determine if it's a blockage or damaged pipe.

How long will the work take?

This can depend on the length and depth of the pipe, replacement method, access, permits, weather and site conditions. A small and accessible project may be quicker to complete than a deep or lengthy replacement. Ask what the timescale will be for your project.

What affects the cost of sewer line replacement in Rockingham, NC?

The cost can depend on the length, depth, material, damage, access, equipment, permits and reinstatement. Trenchless and excavation will have different requirements. The best way to understand what the cost will be is to ask for a written quotation based on the findings of the inspection.

Should I stop using water if sewage is backing up in my home?

Yes. Try to avoid using any water as this can make the backflow worse. Keep people and pets away from any contaminated areas and get professional help. Don't attempt to clear what you think is a failure in your main sewer line using chemical drain cleaners.
