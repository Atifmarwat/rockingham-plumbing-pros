Common Toilet Issues

Being aware of common toilet issues can help property owners understand when a professional may be needed.

Running Toilet

A running toilet can cause issues with excess water being added to the tank/bowl. A common cause can be a flapper that is not sealing correctly. Other causes can be a faulty fill valve, incorrect float adjustment or issues with the overflow tube.

To fix a running toilet you will generally want to look at the internal parts of your toilet tank. A toilet repair kit, universal toilet repair kit or toilet tank repair kit may be relevant depending on the issue and toilet model. Once the cause has been identified the appropriate part can be adjusted or replaced.

Leaking Toilet

Toilets can leak from different areas. This can be from the tank, water supply, toilet tank to toilet bowl connection or base. A leaking water supply can cause water to leak from your toilet. Damage to parts of the tank or a worn seal can also cause leaking.

It is important to know where the water is coming from. Water can run along surfaces and cause pooling on the floor. The connections and toilet should be checked, including the toilet flange repair and toilet flange repair ring if the issue is related to the base connection.

Slow / Incomplete Flushing

A toilet that is not flushing correctly may not be removing waste effectively. This can be due to low water levels in the tank, issues with the flushing mechanism, build up of minerals in the water pathways or a partial blockage.

Depending on the cause of the issue the toilet can be fixed by adjusting a part or if there is a blockage then toilet unclogging can be completed as part of clogged toilet repair.

Toilet Not Flushing / Refilling

If your toilet isn't flushing this can be due to a loose chain on the handle, damaged lever, faulty flush valve or other internal issues. If the toilet isn't refilling after a flush then this can be due to the fill valve or water supply.

Before completing repairs it is a good idea to check that there is water in the tank and that the water supply valve is open. If there is an ongoing issue then you may need a professional to check the internal mechanism and water supply.

Leaking Toilet Base / Loose Toilet

Water leaking from the base of your toilet can be due to a faulty seal, loose toilet or other plumbing issues. If your toilet is rocking then this can cause stress on the connection and seal. Toilet flange repair, toilet repair flange or toilet flange repair kit may be considered if the flange or base connection is damaged.

Continuing to use your toilet can cause more issues. Check the toilet to see if it needs to be secured, sealed or more extensive repairs.

Toilet Repairs

Toilet repairs can be due to individual parts, connections or issues with the toilet flushing and drainage. Toilet repair parts may include compatible valves, flappers, seals, handles or other components needed for toilet bowl repair.

Flush Valve, Flapper & Fill Valve

The flush valve helps control the flow of water from the tank to the bowl. The flapper or seal will help control the flow. The fill valve controls the refill of the tank.

If any of these parts are worn or not working correctly then you may get issues with your toilet running, not refilling or inconsistent flushing. The issue can be resolved by adjusting the mechanism or replacing with a compatible part.

Handle, Chain & Internal Parts

If your toilet handle is loose or the chain has come off then you may have issues flushing your toilet. Other internal parts can also become misaligned or worn.

The correct repair can be completed by adjusting the chain, securing the handle or replacing a damaged lever. Once complete you should check that the handle is working to flush your toilet.

Seal & Connections

The seals and connections on your toilet will help prevent water from leaking. If the seal has become faulty then you may get leaking from your toilet. If the water supply connection has become damaged then you may get leaking from near your tank.

Identify which connection is causing the issue. Depending on the issue the seal can be replaced, connection corrected or the toilet position can be adjusted. Don't assume it is leaking from the base as there may be an issue with the plumbing.

Toilet Unclogging

Toilets can become blocked due to too much toilet paper, inappropriate items going down the toilet bowl or a blockage within the internal parts or drainage. Toilet unclogging aims to clear the blockage and can be part of clogged toilet repair.

Depending on the type and location of the blockage the correct unclogging method can be completed. Don't flush repeatedly if the toilet bowl is nearly full. If you are having frequent blockages then this may be an issue with more than just the toilet and you may need to look at the drainage.

Toilet Repairs or Replacements

If your toilet is suffering from severe damage or ongoing issues then it may be more appropriate to replace your toilet. Consider the condition of your toilet and if there are compatible parts.

Cracked toilets can be tricky to repair and can cause a risk of leaking. The toilet should be assessed.

How Toilet Repairs Work

Generally repairs will involve checking, diagnosing, fixing and testing. Depending on the type of toilet and issue the following can be completed.

Checking & Diagnosing

The toilet will be checked to see what issues there are. This will involve looking at the visible parts of the toilet such as the internal parts of the tank, water supply, flush handle, toilet bowl, base and surrounding plumbing.

This will help identify if there is an issue with the mechanism, leaking, blockage or other parts of the plumbing. This will help ensure that you aren't replacing parts that are still working correctly.

Fixing or Replacing

Once the cause has been identified the correct repair can be completed. This can involve adjusting the internal mechanism, replacing a worn part, correcting a connection or clearing a blockage.

The parts used should be compatible with the toilet model. If the toilet has structural damage or the issue can't be resolved with a practical repair then replacing may be discussed.

Testing

Once the repairs have been completed the toilet should be tested. This can involve flushing the toilet, checking that the toilet is refilling, checking the flush mechanism and connections.

This will ensure that the part has been fixed and the issue is resolved. If the toilet is still not working correctly further diagnosis may be needed.

Things to do Before Requesting Repairs

A few simple things can help prevent further issues.

  • If your toilet is overflowing don't flush and turn off the water supply valve if possible.
  • If there is visible leaking don't use until the cause has been identified.
  • Ensure the area is accessible.
  • Note if the issues happen every time you flush or occasionally.
  • Is the toilet bowl emptying? Is the handle working?
  • Don't force the handle or tighten fragile parts.

If water is pooling on the floor try to protect any items nearby and resolve the cause. If you think there is a blockage don't flush repeatedly as this can cause the toilet bowl to overflow.

Avoid Common Toilet Issues

By keeping an eye on your toilet you may be able to spot any developing issues.

Don't flush anything that shouldn't go down the toilet. Items such as wipes, paper towels, sanitary products and other inappropriate items can cause blockages even if labelled as suitable for disposal elsewhere.

If you notice your toilet is running constantly, leaking from connections or there are issues with the flush then don't ignore it. If you are having to flush multiple times or getting frequent blockages then early assessment can help identify if it is an easy fix with a part or affecting the drainage.

Don't apply too much pressure to the toilet handle. Don't use your toilet as support. If your toilet is rocking, leaking or showing signs of cracking then it should be assessed.
