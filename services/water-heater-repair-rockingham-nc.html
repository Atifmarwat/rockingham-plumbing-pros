Common Water Heater Repairs

Water heaters can have a number of different common issues. You may be experiencing an issue with how much hot water you are getting, or you may have a leak or temperature issues. We can help determine what is causing the issue with your water heater and whether hot water heater repair is required.

No Hot Water

Depending on whether you have an electric or gas water heater there could be a number of reasons you are not receiving any hot water. There could be an issue with the heating element, thermostat, circuitry or wiring. In gas water heaters there could be an issue with the pilot light, burner, gas or control.

If you are not getting hot water but just warm water then you may not be getting enough heat in the tank. There could be an issue with sediment build-up, thermostat or the water heater may be too small.

The correct repair will depend on the appliance and what is discovered during the inspection. Do not keep trying to reset the trip in the breaker box or tamper with the gas components.

Hot Water Takes Too Long To Heat Up

If the water heater is taking too long to heat up then it may be difficult for multiple people to use hot water. This may be due to an issue with heating, sediment build-up, incorrect temperature or the heater is not big enough.

A qualified technician can check the heating components, controls and how the system is performing. If the heater is working correctly but not providing enough hot water for your home then it may be worth discussing upgrading.

Leaking Water Heater

Do not ignore water leaking from the bottom of your water heater. There could be a leak from the connections, valves, drain or tank. If the tank is leaking then it may be due to internal degradation meaning it may be best to replace it.

Strange noises such as popping, rumbling or banging may be due to sediment build-up in the storage tank. If you are getting rust coloured or discolouration in your hot water then it may be due to corrosion. The cause will need to be investigated.

Common Water Heater Issues

Understanding the cause of the issue will help you understand why some water heaters only need a simple component repair whilst others may require more work.

Heating Element, Thermostat & Electrical Issues

Electric water heaters will commonly have one or more heating elements controlled by thermostats. If there is an issue with the heating element you may not be getting enough hot water or any hot water at all. If there is an issue with the thermostat then you may experience temperature issues. Electric water heater repair may involve checking these components, and the required work will depend on the inspection.

You may need to have the connections, wiring and circuitry checked. As water heaters use electricity it is best to have a qualified person carry out any electrical checks or repairs.

Gas Issues, Pilot Light & Burner

Gas water heaters will have a burner and control system. If there is an issue with the pilot light not staying alight, burner or gas control then you may experience issues. Gas water heater repair may involve investigating these components.

Gas issues should be treated with care. If you can smell gas then do not touch any electrical switches or create any flames. Leave the area and call your gas company or emergency services from a safe place. Do not try to troubleshoot the issue.

Sediment Build-Up, Pressure & Leaking Components

Over time minerals can build up in the bottom of the storage tank. This can cause noise, less efficient heating and extra strain on the system. There could also be pressure issues, old valves or degrading connections.

Having a full inspection will help determine if the water heater needs cleaning, component repair or a larger repair.

Gas Water Heater Repairs

Gas water heater repairs may involve checking the pilot, burner, ignition, gas control valve, thermostat, venting and safety components. If the pilot is constantly going out or the heater is not heating it may have an issue that needs to be tested.

It is important to have correct venting and safe burning. If there seems to be any issues with the flame on the burner or venting then stop using the appliance if possible and have a professional look at it.

Electric Water Heater Repairs

Electric water heater repairs may involve testing the heating elements, thermostats, wiring, connections and electrical components. If the heater is constantly tripping the breaker then do not keep turning it back on. Find out what the issue is with the electrical components or appliance. Traditional & Tankless Water Heater Repairs

Traditional storage tanks and tankless heaters heat water differently meaning the components, function and repairs may differ.

Tankless Water Heater Repairs

Tankless water heaters heat water as it passes through the appliance. Issues may include no ignition, low water flow, scaling, sensor issues, error codes or gas/electric issues. Tankless water heater repair will depend on the system and the cause of the problem. If you need tankless water heater repair rockingham nc, an inspection can help identify the issue.

Tankless water heater repairs will involve identifying the issue with the appliance and looking at any diagnostic information. If there is an error code showing then this can help narrow down the issue. Tankless hot water heater repair may involve checking the flow, sensors, heat exchanger and other internal components.

When To Have A Professional Look At Your Tankless Water Heater

If you are experiencing fluctuating temperatures, unexpected shutdown or struggling to get hot water then there may be an issue with the flow, scaling, sensor or internal components. Some systems will also need to have the venting and fuel checked.

Do not open up the unit or override any safety features. Follow the manufacturers guidelines and look at the model.

Water Heater Repairs Or Replacements?

Not all water heaters will be best to repair. If there is a lot of internal degradation or constant failure then you will need to consider the condition, age, repair requirements and hot water requirements of your home.

When To Repair

If the tank is structurally sound and you have an issue with a component that can be repaired such as the thermostat, heating element, valves or connections then it may make sense to repair. If the system has been running well but needs a reasonable amount of repair then it may be worth repairing.

The technician will be able to explain what has failed, what the repair will fix and if there are any other visible issues that may affect performance in the future.

When To Replace

If you have a leaking storage tank due to corrosion, there is a lot of damage or you are constantly having to repair then it may be best to replace. If the water heater is not meeting your homes requirements then it may be worth discussing replacement.

Consider the condition of your current system and the cost of the repairs. If the tank is leaking due to internal degradation then it generally won't be able to be fixed by replacing an external fitting.

Maintenance

Carrying out maintenance on your water heater can help identify any developing issues. Depending on the appliance and manufactures guidelines you may need to check the connections, safety components, anode rod (if applicable) and scaling/mineral build-up.

Tankless systems may need descaling periodically if there is an issue with mineral build-up. How often this needs doing will depend on the model, water and usage. Follow the manufacturers guidelines.

FAQ

Why is my water heater not giving me any hot water?

There could be a number of reasons. This could be due to a failed heating element, thermostat issue, pilot light or burner issue, electrical issue or fuel disruption. This will depend on whether you have a gas or electric unit and will need to be confirmed by inspection.

Can I repair a leaking water heater?

Some leaks may be able to be repaired as they could be from the connections, valves or fittings. If the leaking is due to a corroded storage tank then it will generally need to be replaced. The cause of the leak will need to be identified.

How do I know if I need to replace my water heater?

If there is a leak from the tank, there is a lot of corrosion, it keeps breaking down or it is not meeting your homes requirements then it May Be Worth Considering a replacement. An inspection can help determine the condition and repair requirements of your current unit against a suitable replacement.

Can my water heater make noises but still work?

Yes. You may still be getting hot water but there may be popping or rumbling noises. This could be due to scaling but you should have the appliance checked to see if any maintenance or repairs are needed.

Are tankless water heater repairs different?

Yes. Tankless systems use flow sensors, electrical controls and heat exchangers rather than a traditional tank. This means the diagnosis and repairs will be different.

Should I turn my water heater off if it is leaking?

If it is safe to do so turn the system off. Follow the manufacturers guidelines for turning off. Do not get near any wet electrical components. If there is a smell of gas then leave the area and call the appropriate emergency or gas service from a safe location.

How often should I maintain my water heater?

This will depend on the model, water and manufacturers guidelines. Storage tanks and tankless will need different maintenance. Check the owners manual or ask a qualified technician.
