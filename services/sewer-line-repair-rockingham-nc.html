Common Issues That Cause Damage to Your Sewer Line

There are a number of reasons that sewer pipes can degrade or become blocked. It is important to identify the cause of the issue as if there is a blockage it will not resolve a structural issue.

Tree Roots

Tree roots will naturally grow towards sources of moisture. If there is already a crack, loose joint or opening in your sewer pipe then tree roots can grow into the pipe. This can cause a reduction in wastewater flow over time.

If there are frequent blockages then it may be worth having a closer look at your sewer line to see if tree roots are causing the issue. Whilst removing the roots may resolve the issue temporarily, the opening or damaged section may require repairs.

Old, Cracked or Collapsed Pipes

Over time older sewer pipes can degrade. This can be due to the material degrading, corrosion (if applicable) and joints failing. This can cause cracks and loose joints meaning wastewater can escape into the surrounding soil.

In more severe cases the pipe may become distorted and collapsed meaning no wastewater can pass through. As collapsed pipes cannot be returned to their original form with cleaning equipment it may be necessary to repair or replace.

Blockages & Ground Movement

Sewer lines can become blocked due to grease, foreign objects, build up and other debris. It can also be caused by ground movement, soil conditions, nearby construction and changes to the load above your underground pipes.

By having a professional assessment it can help identify if there is a blockage that can be removed.

Camera Inspection

Camera inspections use specialised cameras attached to equipment that can travel through an accessible section of your sewer pipe. This allows the technician to view the inside of the pipe on a screen.

This may help to identify:

  • Cracks and visible damage.
  • Roots and build up.
  • Loose joints.
  • Restricted areas.
  • Degradation or collapsed pipes.
  • Approximate location of the blockage.

The results of the camera inspection will depend on how accessible the pipe is, water and visibility. Whilst it can provide useful information it may not identify everything outside of the pipe.

Identify Where Damage Has Occurred

Sewer Line Repairs

Sewer line repairs can help to resolve issues with defects in the pipes. The type of repair will depend on the type of damage, material of the pipe, depth and accessibility. If you are looking for sewer pipe repair near me, the condition and location of the damaged pipe should be assessed first.

Repairs to Cracked or Leaking Pipes

If there is a single crack, damaged joint or leaking section then a local repair may be suitable. Depending on the pipe and damage the affected section can be exposed, removed and replaced with suitable piping and joints.

If there are multiple issues with the pipe or it has significantly degraded then repairing just one visible issue may not provide a long term solution. The condition of the rest of the line should be considered.

Local Repairs

A local repair will focus on repairing a specific section of damage. This may be suitable if the rest of the pipe is in a suitable condition.

Some sewer lines may be able to be repaired using trenchless techniques to reduce the amount of digging required. However this may not be suitable for all pipes and damage. The shape, condition, alignment and accessibility of the existing pipe should be considered.

Ask questions to understand what repairs are being carried out, what section is being repaired and if the rest of the line has been inspected.

Considerations for Replacement

You may consider replacement if:

Considerations Before Replacing

Avoid Future Issues

FAQ'S

How do I know if my sewer line needs repairing?

Multiple drains backing up, recurring blockages, sewage smells and wastewater appearing from floor drains may indicate an issue with your sewer line. An inspection can help to identify if there is a blockage or structural issue.

What does a sewer line inspection include in Rockingham?

An inspection may include looking at accessible plumbing connections, drainage issues and using a sewer camera to view inside the pipe. The process will depend on what is suspected and how accessible the line is.

Should I repair or replace my sewer line?

If there is a small amount of damage to a section of pipe and the rest of the pipe is in good condition then repairs may be suitable. If there is a lot of damage, the pipe has collapsed or repairs have not resolved the issue then replacement may be worth considering.

Can sewer drain cleaning repair a damaged sewer pipe?

No. Whilst sewer drain cleaning can help to clear certain blockages it will not repair a cracked, disconnected or collapsed pipe. If there is structural damage then repairs may be required.

Why am I getting recurring blockages?

This can be due to root intrusion, build up of grease & debris, foreign objects, misaligned pipes and degraded sections. An inspection may help to identify why you are getting recurring blockages.

Can I always have trenchless sewer line repairs?

No. Trenchless repairs will depend on the condition, shape, accessibility and type of damage to the existing pipe. Some situations will require traditional digging.

Do I need permits for new sewer line installation?

This will depend on the project, property and local authority requirements. Ensure you know what permits, approvals and inspections are required.
