Common Bathtub Issues That May Require Repairs

Many issues can occur with your bathtub. These can be with your plumbing, drain assembly, faucet, controls or surface. Some of these issues will be obvious to spot whilst others will show signs of regular dampness, strange noises or changes to your water flow.

Leaking Bathtub Faucet Or Spout

If your bathtub faucet is dripping then it could be due to worn parts, damaged seals or faulty valve. Bathtub faucet repair may be required if the handle is loose, the temperature fluctuates or there is water leaking from the faucet then there could be an issue with the fixture or connections.

This can cause excess water wastage. The cause of the dripping will determine the fixing required. The design of your faucet and condition of the cartridge, washers, seals or valve will dictate the repair.

Water escaping from around the spout or trim could be due to an issue with one of the connections or plumbing.

Slow Bathtub Draining

Your bathtub could be slow to drain due to build up of hair, soap scum or other debris. This could also be due to a damaged stopper or drain.

Symptoms include:

  • Water pooling around your feet when showering
  • Slow draining
  • Gurgling from the drain
  • Unpleasant smells from the drain
  • Stopper not opening/closing or sealing

The issue could be resolved by removing any debris visible, fixing the stopper or looking further into the issue. If you are regularly having issues with the drain getting blocked then this could require further investigation.

Cracking Or Chipping

Over time your bathtub may start to chip, scratch, crack or become worn. This can happen due to impact, age, incorrect cleaning products or movement.

A small chip in the finish can be different to a crack in the actual bathtub. Some surface issues can be repaired using specialist products, such as a bathtub chip repair kit or bathtub repair kit. A bathtub crack repair kit may be suitable for certain surface cracks. However if there are cracks in the bathtub then this may affect its ability to hold water.

Depending on the material of your bathtub, the way it can be repaired may differ. For example, an acrylic bathtub may not be able to be repaired in the same way as a porcelain enamel or fibreglass bathtub. Fiberglass bathtub repair may require products suited to the specific material, including a fiberglass bathtub repair kit.

Damaged / Leaking Drain Assembly

The drain assembly on your bathtub contains parts to help manage the water flow. Depending on the design this could include a drain flange, stopper, chain, overflow plate and plumbing.

If your bathtub is leaking around the drain then the water could be escaping underneath. If you're noticing water on your ceiling (if it's an upstairs bathroom) or around the flooring then this could be due to an issue with the drain connections. It could also be due to an issue with other areas of the plumbing. Bathtub leak repair will depend on identifying the source of the leak.

Before sealing any areas the cause should be identified. Simply replacing a visible part of the drain will not necessarily fix the issue with the hidden connections.

Issues With Controls

The controls on your bathtub are used to manage the water flow. If the handle is difficult to turn, the valve isn't working or the temperature of the water fluctuates then there could be an issue with worn or faulty parts.

This could be due to a damaged cartridge, worn seals, faulty valve or an issue with the water supply. Having the issue assessed will help determine if it's just an issue with the bathtub or wider plumbing.

Bathtub Material

The repair should be done in accordance with the material and finish of your bathtub. It's important to know what material your bathtub is made from.

Acrylic Or Fibreglass

These are commonly found in residential bathrooms. They are light weight however they can become scratched, chipped, stained or cracked. Fiberglass bathtub repair should use products suitable for the material and the type of damage.

If there is minor surface damage then using the correct repair compound or refinishing could be suitable. If there is more damage then depending on the material of your bathtub reinforcement may be required.

A professional will be able to assess whether the area is stable and whether it's well supported. Fixing the crack may not solve the issue if it's due to movement.

Porcelain Enamel / Steel

Some bathtubs have a metal base with a porcelain enamel finish. This gives a smooth surface however can chip easily when impacted.

If there are small chips then using the correct repair product for the surface may be suitable. However if there is a lot of damage, corrosion or repeated damage then this could require more extensive repair.

Using cleaning products and abrasive tools can cause damage. Using the correct products for your bathtub will help prevent worsening any wear.

Cast Iron

These are heavy bathtubs and usually have a tough enamel finish. They can still become chipped, worn and have damage to the finish. Due to the weight and setup of the bathtub it can be more involved to remove and replace.

The repair should take into consideration the surface and condition of your bathtub. If there is a lot of wear to the finish then refinishing could be an option if the structure is suitable.

Common Bathtub Repairs

Depending on the issue with your bathtub the correct solution will depend on the material and whether it's an issue with the bathtub or connections.

Faucet, Handle And Valve Repairs

If there is an issue with your faucet then this can be fixed by replacing any worn seals, washers, cartridges, handles or other suitable parts. In some cases the whole fixture may need replacing.

When working on the valve you should take note of the plumbing. Ensure that the type of fixture is suitable and compatible.

Drain And Stopper Repairs

If there is an issue with your drain then this can be resolved by unblocking, adjusting or replacing the stopper. The drain can be repaired or connections fixed.

The overflow should also be taken into consideration. If there is an issue with the sealing or damage then water can escape. This may not be an issue with the main drain.

If there is an ongoing blockage then further investigation may be required.

Cracking / Chipping

Some chipping can be repaired using products to suit the specific finish of your bathtub. Bathtub chip repair may involve using a suitable repair product or bathtub chip repair kit. The area should be cleaned and prepared before filling, curing and finishing.

Depending on the size, depth, surrounding material and product used the results may vary. Surface repairs shouldn't be seen as a guaranteed fix if the bathtub is compromised.

Investigating Leaks

There could be a leak from your faucet, drain, overflow, supply connection or surrounding plumbing. Water can seep behind the walls or under the floor meaning the cause may not be where the damp is showing.

An investigation may be required to check the connections, bathtub and surrounding areas. If there has been any damage to surrounding materials due to the moisture then this may need attention.

What To Expect From A Professional Bathtub Repair

Depending on the issue you're experiencing with your bathtub the repair will vary. However having a clear assessment will help ensure you're not just fixing the symptoms.

1. Issue Assessed

The professional will discuss the issue you're having, check the bathtub and any visible fixtures. They will ask when the issue is happening. This can help narrow down the cause. For example, knowing when the leak is happening or if the drain is regularly blocked.

2. Find Cause

They will look at the relevant faucet, drain, overflow, surface or plumbing. If the cause is hidden or there is a lot of damage then further investigation may be required.

3. Plan Repair

Depending on the material, condition and availability of compatible parts the professional will plan the repair. They should be able to tell you whether it looks like it can be repaired and if any other work may be required.

4. Carry Out Repair

Depending on the issue, parts of the fixture will be replaced, connections to the drain fixed, blockage cleared or surface damage repaired.

5. Check Repairs

Ensure that everything is working correctly. Check the water flow, drainage, fixtures and visible connections.

Bathtub Repair Or Installation

If your bathtub is structurally sound and the issue is with a part or area that can be repaired then it's often worth repairing. For example, if the stopper is faulty, the faucet is dripping, there is minor chipping or there is an issue with the drain then you may not need to replace.

However if there is a lot of cracking, corrosion, regular leaking or damage to the structure of your bathtub then it may be better to replace. If the bathtub doesn't fit with the layout or accessibility requirements of your bathroom then this could also be an option.

Bathtub Installation Rockingham NC is a separate service. This could involve replacing the current bathtub, preparing the area, checking the plumbing, placing the new bathtub and connecting to the drain and water. If you are considering a replacement then you should consider the size of your bathroom, access through the doorway, material of the bathtub and plumbing.

Take into consideration the condition of your bathtub and what work is required. Comparing the options will help ensure you aren't spending money on a repair that won't fix the issue.

Cost Of Bathtub Repairs

The cost of a repair will depend on what damage has occurred, material, accessibility and parts. Minor issues with the faucet will be different to investigating hidden leaks or repairing damage.

Common reasons for cost variations include:

  • Type Of Issue – Depending on whether it's an issue with the faucet, drain, valve, surface or structure the method will vary.
  • Material – Depending on whether it's acrylic, fibreglass, porcelain enamel or cast iron the products may vary.
  • Amount Of Damage – If there isn't much damage then it may not be as much work as if there is a large crack or lots of wear to the finish.
  • Accessibility Of Plumbing – If the connections are hidden then investigating leaks could be more involved.
  • Parts Required – Availability and compatibility of cartridges, drain parts, handles etc.
  • Other Damage – If there has been damage to flooring, wall materials or surrounding areas due to water then this may require repair.

Before authorising any work ask what is included in the quotation. Are any parts required to be replaced? How will any other damage be dealt with?

When To Book A Professional Bathtub Repair

Some issues with your bathtub should be assessed promptly. Especially if there is water escaping or damage getting worse.

Consider booking an inspection if you're noticing:

  • Water leaking around your bathtub or in the adjoining room
  • Growing crack or water escaping
  • Dripping faucet
  • Regularly blocked drain
  • Loose fixtures or movement around the drain and overflow
  • Soft, discoloured or damp materials

If there is active leaking then stop using your bathtub until it can be assessed. If it's safe to do so turn off the relevant water supply and protect any items around. Don't attempt to seal any unknown leaks.

Maintaining Your Repaired Bathtub

Looking after your bathtub can help keep the finish in good condition. Follow the cleaning advice from the manufacturer and don't use abrasive tools or harsh products.

Good tips include:

  • Cleaning regularly with a product suitable for your bathtub.
  • Removing hair and debris from the drain.
  • Don't drop heavy objects onto the surface.
  • Check the handles, spouts and visible connections for any signs of leaking.
  • Keep an eye on the sealant.
  • Don't ignore loose fixtures or drainage issues.

Sealing is important however caulk shouldn't be used as a substitute for fixing a leaking pipe, drain or failed waterproofing connection. If there is continued moisture then investigate the cause.

FAQs

Can Cracked Bathtubs Be Repaired?

Depending on the size, depth, location and material of your bathtub some cracks can be repaired. If it's a surface issue then it may be suitable to use specialist products, such as a bathtub crack repair kit. If it's affecting the structure of the bathtub or causing water to escape then it may need replacing. An assessment will be required.

Can Bathtub Drains Be Repaired?

Yes! Many drain issues are due to the stopper, drain assembly, overflow or connections. Depending on the location and condition of the parts the correct repair can be carried out.

Is A Dripping Faucet A Plumbing Issue?

It can be. Worn washers, seals, cartridges or valve parts could be causing the dripping. Depending on the design and condition of the faucet some parts can be repaired or the fixture may need replacing.

Can Chipped Bathtubs Be Fixed?

If there are small chips then they may be able to be repaired using products suitable for your bathtub. If there is a large area of damage then a more extensive repair may be required. Ensure the product matches your bathtub.

Should I Repair Or Replace My Old Bathtub?

Depending on how damaged it is, whether it's structurally sound, how often there are issues and how much the repair will cost and how extensive it will be. If it's structurally sound and there is a small issue then it may be practical to repair. If there is a lot of damage then it may be worth considering replacing.

Why Is Water Leaking Around My Drain?

This could be due to loose or damaged connections, worn seals or an issue with the plumbing underneath. It could also be due to an issue with the overflow or other connection. Before replacing any visible parts the cause should be identified.

Can All Damage Be Fixed By Refinishing?

No. Refinishing can help improve the appearance of some worn or discoloured areas however it may not fix structural cracks, leaking plumbing or an unstable bathtub. An assessment should be carried out.

How Can I Prevent My Bathtub From Getting Blocked?

Remove any visible hair and debris. Clean around the stopper. Don't let too much soap scum build up. If your drain is regularly slowing down despite regular cleaning then it could be further up the drain line and may require professional attention.
