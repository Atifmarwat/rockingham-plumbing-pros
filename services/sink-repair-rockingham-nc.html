Common Issues With Sinks

Sinks are used throughout the day and so the plumbing can suffer from constant exposure to water, cleaning products, food waste and general wear and tear. There are a number of issues that could be causing the problem with your sink.

Leaking Tap or Fittings

Having water leaking from your tap, under the sink or from your drain can cause issues. There could be a number of reasons for this including worn tap parts, loose fittings, worn seals, broken supply lines or bad drain fittings.

Common signs of this issue include:

  • Water dripping from the tap
  • Wet around the base of the tap
  • Pooling water in the cabinet under the sink
  • Wet supply lines or drain
  • Water marks on the countertops.

Knowing where the water is leaking from can help identify the issue. If it is coming from the tap then there may be issues with the inner workings. If it is leaking from under the sink there may be an issue with your water supply or drain. An inspection will help to identify.

Blocked or Slow Draining

Having a slow draining sink can be due to blockages in the drain hole, stopper or drain pipe. Kitchen sinks can cause issues with food waste and oil while bathroom sinks can cause issues with hair, soap scum and personal care products.

Signs of this issue include:

  • Water pooling in the sink
  • Gurgling from the drain
  • Bad smells
  • Clogging regularly
  • Water backing up in the sink.

If it is a localised blockage then it may not be too much of an issue however if it is regularly clogging then there may be a larger issue with your plumbing.

Damaged Basin

Over time basins can start to crack, chip, stain and degrade. This can cause issues with the appearance and in some cases the ability to hold water.

Depending on the material of your sink, minor damages may not require any plumbing work. If there is a crack allowing water to leak then there may be a bigger issue.

Depending on the material of the sink (porcelain, ceramic, stainless steel, composite etc.) there may be limitations on what can be done. Some basins can be repaired while others may need replacing. Cracked sink repair may be possible depending on the material and extent of the damage.

Loose Sink

If your sink is moving when pressure is applied there could be an issue with loose fittings, degraded support or damaged seals.

This can happen with under mount, drop in and other types of sinks. If your sink is loose it should be assessed to see what the issue may be. This can cause stress on your drain fittings, supply lines and the surrounding countertop.

Fixing this issue may involve tightening your fittings, fixing the support or replacing the seal. It will depend on the type of sink you have.

Why Are You Having Issues With Your Sink Plumbing?

Knowing why you are having issues with your sink can help determine what repairs are needed. Common issues include:

  • Wear and Tear – Cartridges, washers, seals and more can wear.
  • Build up – Food waste, oil, hair and soap scum can build up in the drain and pipes.
  • Loose fittings – Vibrations, repeated use and incorrect fit can cause fittings to become loose.
  • Corrosion – Metal parts can corrode especially if they are exposed to water for long periods.
  • Incorrect Installation – Incorrectly fitted drain, incorrect connections and lack of support can cause ongoing issues.
  • Damage – Impact, too much pressure and incorrect cleaning products can cause damage.
  • Old Plumbing – Old pipes and fittings can cause leaks, blockages and other issues.

Not all issues will have one cause. For example, water pooling under your sink can be due to leaking from the tap supply lines, drain fittings, seal on the basin or other nearby plumbing. An inspection will help.

Sink Repairs Vs Installation

Sink repairs will be to repair an existing sink and its components. This can include fixing leaks, clearing blockages, fixing drain connections, repairing tap components and tightening your loose basin.

Installation of a sink is fitting a new sink into your countertop, cabinet or other area. This may be needed if you are doing renovations, upgrading or replacing your sink.

If you are looking for sink installation in Rockingham NC then you may need a new sink. Installation can include checking the measurements, fitting, drain alignment, tap compatibility and supply lines.

If your existing sink is still in a good structural condition then repairs may be suitable. If your basin is badly cracked, the sink isn't suitable for the area or there is lots of degradation then it may be better to install/replace.

A professional can help you decide what the best option is for your sink and property.

When To Request Professional Sink Repairs

Some issues can be resolved by simple cleaning, however some may need a professional.

Consider requesting a professional if:

How To Look After Your Sink

Looking after your sink can help reduce the chance of common issues.

  • Don't pour any grease, oils or food waste down your kitchen drain.
  • Use a suitable drain strainer.
  • Clear any hair and build up from your bathroom drain holes.
  • Clean any restricted tap aerators.
  • Check any visible supply and drain lines for any wetness.
  • Don't force your tap handles or drain stopper.
  • Use suitable cleaning products for your sink.
  • Fix any small leaks.
  • Follow manufacturers guidelines.

Not all plumbing issues can be prevented however looking after your sink can help identify any developing issues and reduce avoidable wear.

FAQ

What are the most common issues with sink repairs?

Common issues include leaking taps, blocked drains, damaged drain, loose sink, leaking supply lines and degraded seals. The correct repairs will depend on what is causing the issue.

Can I repair a leaking sink?

In most cases yes. If the basin is still in a good structural condition then the leaking could be due to a repairable tap part, supply line, drain fitting or seal. Knowing what is causing it will help determine if it needs replacing.

Why is my sink draining slowly?

This can be due to hair, oil, soap scum, food waste and build up in the drain and pipes. If it is regularly draining slowly then it may need a more in depth assessment.

Can I repair a cracked sink?

Depending on the material of your sink and where the crack is some minor surface damage may be repairable. If there is a crack allowing water to leak or affecting the structural integrity then you may need to replace your sink.

Why is there water leaking under my kitchen sink?

This can be due to leaking from your supply line, shutoff valve, tap, drain, P trap or other nearby parts. Knowing what is causing it will help ensure you don't do the wrong repairs.

When should I install a new sink?

If your current basin is badly damaged, the sink isn't suitable for the area or you are doing renovations then installing a new sink may be suitable. If your current sink is in a good condition then repairs may be enough.

Can I repair a blocked sink without replacing the pipes?

In many cases localised blockages can be cleared. However if there is damaged, degraded or incorrectly fitted plumbing then this may cause extra work.
