Common Issues

Your tankless water heater can suffer from various issues depending on what is causing the problem.

No Hot Water Or Temperature Issues

This can be caused by the tankless water heater not providing hot water due to an issue with the ignition, interruption to the energy source, sensor issue or control board. With electric models there can be issues with the heating elements and electricity.

Temperature issues can occur if the tankless water heater isn’t able to provide the right amount of hot water. This can be due to fluctuating water flow, high demand, scale build up, temperature sensor issues or incorrect settings.

If your hot water is going from hot to lukewarm then there should be an investigation into why this is happening. A technician may need to look into whether it is an issue with the unit, plumbing or too many appliances in use.

Low Hot Water Flow

The tankless water heater may not be providing enough hot water. This can be due to a blockage in the water supply, dirty inlet filters, scale build up or issues with the plumbing.

Some tankless water heaters require a minimum flow of water to start heating. If there isn’t enough water flowing through the unit to reach the manufacturers minimum flow then the burner/heating elements will not kick in.

A professional can help identify if the issue is with the water heater, appliance or plumbing.

Error Codes

Many tankless water heaters will show error codes when there is an issue with the system. Depending on the make and model of your water heater the error could be due to ignition issues, temperature sensor issues, low airflow, overheating or something else.

Whilst the error code can be used to start troubleshooting it may not show what the issue. The technician should refer to the service manual for the model and check the condition. It is not always the case that the error code means a component needs replacing.

If the system is constantly cutting out then this shouldn’t be ignored.

Leaking

Even though tankless water heaters don’t have a large tank they can still suffer from leaking. This can occur due to issues with the plumbing, isolation valve, pressure components or internal components.

Depending on where the leak is coming from and how severe it is there can be different repairs. If the leak is coming from a loose/damaged connection then it will be different to if it is leaking from the heat exchanger.

If the leaking is coming from near any electrical components then only turn the appliance off if it is safe to do so. Do not touch any wet electrical components. If the leak is significant then it should be sorted quickly.

Why Are There Issues With My Tankless Water Heater?

Knowing what could be causing the issues can help.

Scale Build Up

As water flows through the heat exchanger scale can build up. This can cause a blockage in the water flow.

Depending on your system and water you may be experiencing temperature issues, poor performance, strange noises or overheating due to the scale build up.

A descaling service can be carried out on your tankless water heater. This involves flushing out the heat exchanger with the correct descaling solution. This will depend on the manufacturer guidelines and may involve using isolation valves and specific equipment for the model.

This will not always be the case. If there is an issue with the sensors, heating components or something else then it won’t be solved by just descaling.

Gas, Ignition & Electrical Issues

Tankless water heaters that run on gas will need to have the correct fuel source and ignition. There can be issues with the gas supply, ignition electrode, flame detection, gas valve or other components causing the burner not to work. These issues may require tankless gas water heater repair.

Electric models will need to have the correct electricity supply and heating elements. There can be issues with the wiring, breakers, heating elements or control components.

Due to the gas and electricity there should be a professional with the right qualifications to diagnose and repair. The repair should be done as per manufacturer requirements and local codes.

Ventilation Issues

Gas tankless water heaters will require the correct ventilation. If there is a blockage, damage, incorrect set up or something else then this can cause issues. The appliance may cut out.

Investigate the cause of the issue.

Depending on the design, set up and manufacturer guidelines the correct checks can be completed.

Gas & Electric Tankless Water Heater Repairs

Tankless water heaters run on different heating methods so there can be differences in diagnosis and repairs.

Gas models will use a burner to heat the water via the heat exchanger. There can be issues with the ignition, flame detection, gas supply, burning, ventilation, sensors and water flow. If there is an issue with the gas then it needs to be carefully diagnosed as there are safety issues with incorrect burning and fuel.

Electric models will use heating elements to transfer heat to the water. There can be issues with the electricity supply, heating elements, temperature sensors, controls or water flow. Some models will have high electrical requirements so it is important to safely diagnose.

Both can suffer from scaling, blockages, sensor issues or control issues. The exact repair will depend on the model and issue.

When requesting a repair it is best to know what make, model, fuel type and error code (if any) the water heater has.

Tankless Water Heater Repairs Or Replacements

If the tankless water heater is in good condition and the issue can be repaired then it may be a good option to repair. If there is a lot of damage, constant issues or it isn’t economical to repair then it may be worth replacing.

Factors to consider:

  • Age & Condition – How old is the unit? Has it been well maintained? What is the overall condition?
  • Repair – Is it a small issue with the sensor or a big issue with the heat exchanger?
  • Replacement Parts – Are there replacement parts available from the manufacturer?
  • Constant Issues – Is there constant issues? This can be an indication there is a bigger issue or the unit is not in good condition.
  • Hot Water Demand – If the system is constantly not providing enough hot water for the property then it may be worth looking at the capacity.
  • Installation – If considering a replacement then you may need to look at the ventilation, water connections and electrical/gas requirements.

Overall the decision should be based on an assessment of the appliance and how it is running. Simply because it is showing an error code doesn’t mean it needs replacing. If the unit is very run down and constantly needing repair then it may not be the most practical option.

Prevention & Maintenance

Regular maintenance can help reduce the chance of some issues occurring with your tankless water heater. This will depend on the manufacturers guidelines, water quality, how much it is used and how it is installed.

Regular Descaling & Filters

Scaling can cause issues with the heat exchanger. If you live in an area or have water with lots of minerals then descaling regularly can help keep the heat transfer and water flowing.

Many systems will have inlet filters which can get dirty. By following the manufacturers guidelines to clean or service them can help reduce blockages.

Do not use the wrong cleaning solution or attempt to clean it yourself. Refer to the appliance guidelines.

Monitor Performance

If you notice any changes with the hot water, strange noises, error codes or water flow then this can be an indication there is an issue developing.

Keep the maintenance guidelines from the manufacturer and note any previous repairs. This can be useful when diagnosing constant issues.

Ensure the area is suitable. The area should be easily accessible and have the correct clearances as per the manufacturers guidelines. Gas models will need to have the correct burning air and ventilation.

Do not store anything that will prevent you getting to the appliance. Never attempt to alter the ventilation or safety controls.

Urgent Repairs

Some issues with your tankless water heater may need urgent attention.

If you believe you have a gas or propane leak then leave the building immediately. Don’t touch any switches or cause any ignition. Contact your gas company or emergency services from a safe distance.

If you have a carbon monoxide alarm then go outside and contact emergency services. Do not go back in until it is safe.

If there is water near any electrical components then do not touch any wet electrical components. Contact a qualified professional.

If there is a large amount of water leaking then take the correct steps to protect your property (if it is safe) and get a professional to assess it quickly.

If the system is constantly overheating or cutting out then stop using it. Have the cause looked into.

Not all issues with your tankless water heater will be urgent. If it is simply not providing hot water then it may be worth booking in for a diagnosis. Contact the provider to ensure they are available and how quick they can respond.

FAQ

My tankless water heater isn’t providing hot water, why?

This can be due to an interruption to the gas/electricity, ignition issues, sensor issues, lack of water flow or control issues. The exact cause will depend on the model and issues you are facing. A diagnosis can help identify the right repair.

Can my tankless water heater be repaired?

Yes. Many issues can be repaired. This could include sensor issues, ignition issues, water flow issues and scaling. It will depend on the issue, condition of the appliance and if the right parts are available.

How do I know if my tankless water heater needs descaling?

You may notice a lack of hot water flow, temperature issues, strange noises or certain error codes. However this can be due to other issues. Refer to the manufacturers guidelines for maintenance and have it assessed.

How long will repairs take?

This will depend on the issue, diagnosis and if there are replacement parts. A simple repair may take less time than a complicated intermittent issue or complex component failure. A professional can give you a more realistic time once they have assessed the unit.

Is it worth repairing an old tankless water heater?

This can depend on the issue. If it is a small issue and the system is in good condition then it may be worth it. If there is a lot of damage, constant issues, no replacement parts or expensive repairs then it may be worth replacing. This will depend on the condition of the unit and what repair is required.

My tankless water heater keeps cutting out why?

This can be due to lack of water flow, overheating, ignition issues, ventilation issues, sensor issues or other issues. The error code may give you some useful information but it is important to ensure the issue is what it is before replacing any components.

Do gas and electric tankless water heaters need different repairs?

Yes. Gas models will have a burner, ignition, fuel source and ventilation. Electric models will use heating elements and electrical controls. Both can suffer from water flow issues, scaling and sensor issues. The right repair should be completed.

Should I reset my tankless water heater?

This may be appropriate for certain situations. However if you are constantly resetting it due to it not working then it is not a reliable repair. If the error code comes back then book in for an assessment. Don’t ignore the safety features.
