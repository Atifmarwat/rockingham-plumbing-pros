Common Shower Issues That May Require Repairs

Issues with showers can happen over time due to wear and tear. Understanding what the issues are can help you explain the problem.

Leaking Shower Heads & Fixtures

If your shower head is constantly dripping after turning off the water, this could be due to a worn washer, faulty seal, or build-up of minerals. It can also be due to the shower valve itself. However, sometimes the issue is due to the shower arm not being tight enough or the connection not sealing properly.

Dripping or Faulty Shower Valves

The shower valve controls how much water comes into your shower. Many shower systems also have a valve to control the ratio of hot and cold water. Issues can occur if the shower valve has worn parts causing it to drip, making it difficult to turn the handle, and causing fluctuating water.

Depending on the shower valve, the issue can be resolved by shower valve repair, replacing the cartridge, repairing the seal, or fixing the faulty shower valve. The correct part needs to be used.

Low or Fluctuating Water Pressure

A lack of water pressure in your shower can be an uncomfortable experience. This can be due to a blocked shower head, build-up of minerals, partially closed shutoff valve, or blockage in the water feed.

If cleaning the shower head does not improve the water pressure, the issue may be with the plumbing. Checking other taps can help.

Blocked or Slow Draining Shower

Hair, soap scum, and other debris can build up in your shower drain and shower drain pipe causing it to drain slowly. This can be inconvenient having water sitting in your shower and can mean a blockage is starting to form.

The correct cleaning method will depend on where the blockage is and how bad it is. If blockages are occurring regularly, further investigation may be required to see if it is a shower drain issue or a larger drainage issue.

Water Temperature Issues

If your shower is suddenly getting too hot or too cold and is difficult to control, this can be due to a faulty shower mixer valve and cartridge. However, water temperature can also be affected by the water heater and other plumbing issues.

Before replacing your shower fixture, find out what the cause is. If other hot water sources are also having issues, it may not just be an issue with your shower.

Leaking Shower Pans, Walls & Connections

Leakage from your shower can be due to faulty seals, loose connections, worn grout, and failed caulk. It can also be due to a faulty shower pan or waterproofing. Shower pan repair may be required if the pan itself is damaged.

Not all issues with water leaking around your shower will be a plumbing issue. Water could be leaking due to faulty joins in your shower unit. Before carrying out any aesthetic repairs, find out what the cause is.

Shower Faucet Repair, Tap, Valves & Fixtures Repairs

The shower tap and valves are an important part of your shower. Issues can occur causing problems with your shower. Shower faucet repair may be needed if the tap or its components are faulty.

Common issues include:

  • Worn shower tap cartridges.
  • Dripping shower taps.
  • Loose or stiff handles.
  • Leaking shower arm connections.
  • Damaged shower heads.
  • Faulty mixer valves.
  • Leaking connections.

The correct repair will depend on what brand your shower is, the type of valve, how old it is, and the state of the plumbing. Some shower systems allow individual parts to be replaced. However, others may require a larger shower valve repair.

Using the correct parts is important. Using the wrong shower cartridge or parts that don't fit well can cause issues with the original issue.

Shower Drain & Drainage Repairs

The shower drain should be efficient at removing waste water. If you are having issues with slow draining, this could be due to a blockage close to the drain or further down the drain pipe.

To determine what repairs are needed, you may need to check the shower drain cover, strainer, and any visible debris. If there is a blockage, the correct cleaning tools may be needed.

There may also be an issue with the shower drain connection, loose connection, or leaking underneath the shower base. If water is coming through onto the ceiling below an upstairs bathroom or around the surrounding flooring, further investigation may be needed.

Be cautious when using chemical drain cleaners. Some can cause issues with the plumbing. Investigating persistent or recurring blockage would be better.

What To Expect From Professional Shower Repairs

The repair will depend on the issues you are experiencing and type of shower. However, a typical service may include:

1. Talking Through Issues

The engineer will ask when the issue started, is it constant, and if it is affecting any other plumbing. They will want to know if you are experiencing dripping after turning the shower off, fluctuating temperatures, or water pooling around the shower drain.

2. Inspection Of Shower Components

The engineer will inspect the shower head, shower tap, handles, valve, and drain. They will also inspect any visible connections. If there is any water showing outside of the shower unit, they may need to investigate the surrounding area.

3. Determine What Repairs Are Required

Once they have determined what issues you are experiencing, they can explain what the likely cause is and what repairs can be carried out. Some issues can be fixed easily with a small replacement. However, others may require more plumbing work.

4. Carry Out Repairs

The engineer will carry out the repair. This can include replacing the shower cartridge, fixing any connections, clearing any blockages, or repairing faulty parts. Ensure any replacement parts match.

5. Test The Shower

Once repairs have been carried out, the shower should be tested to ensure it is working properly. This can include testing the water flow, temperature, drainage, and ensuring no issues with the shower fixtures or connections.

Shower Repairs vs Installations

Shower repairs will fix issues with your current shower.

Shower repairs are suitable if the shower fixtures, valves, drain, and connections can be fixed.

Shower installations involve installing a new shower or large part of the shower. This can be necessary if you are renovating your bathroom, or there is significant damage to the shower unit or shower base.

If you are looking to carry out a Shower Installation Rockingham NC, you may need to choose a shower base, shower unit, fixtures, and correct connections. Depending on how large the project is, you may also need to waterproof, prepare the walls, and change the layout of your bathroom.

If there is an issue with the shower valve or drain that can be repaired, you may not need a full installation. However, if there is significant damage to the shower unit or underlying structure, you may need a larger replacement. An inspection can help determine what service is best for your bathroom.

When To Get Prompt Repairs

Some shower issues should be repaired as soon as possible to reduce the risk of further damage.

You should consider getting an inspection if:

  • There is water leaking after turning off the shower.
  • There is water on the walls, ceiling, or flooring outside of the shower unit.
  • There is water pooling around the shower base.
  • The shower drain is backing up regularly.
  • The shower tap/valve is becoming difficult to turn.
  • There is sudden changes in water temperature.
  • You can see signs of a leak in the room below.

If there is active water leaking, turn off the relevant water supply if possible. Avoid using the shower until the issue has been assessed and there is no significant leak or electrical issues near the wet area.

How To Reduce Future Issues

Keeping your shower fixtures and drainage clean can help identify issues early.

  • Clean your shower head regularly.
  • Clear any visible hair and debris from the drain cover.
  • Ensure the shower tap handles and connections are not loose.
  • Look out for dripping.
  • Ensure the grout and caulk are in good condition.
  • Investigate any water on the outside of the shower unit.
  • Do not force any stiff shower tap handles or valves.
  • If there are regular drainage issues, try to resolve them.

While regular maintenance won't stop all plumbing failures, it can help identify any leaks, worn parts, or drainage issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my shower dripping?

This can be due to a worn shower cartridge, faulty seal, and faulty shower valve. The exact issue will depend on the shower tap. Before replacing any parts ensure it is checked.

Can I repair my leaking shower?

Yes. Many leaks will be due to replaceable parts. However, if there is damage to the shower pan, shower unit, or hidden plumbing lines more work may be required.

Why is my water pressure low?

Your shower head may be blocked, there could be build-up of minerals, or there may be an issue with the water feeds. Checking other taps can help determine if it is an issue with your shower.

Why is my shower draining slowly?

Hair, soap scum, and other debris can often build up in shower drains. If the issue isn't resolved by cleaning the shower drain, it may be further down the line.

Should I repair or replace my old shower valve?

If there is a replaceable cartridge or seal it could be repaired. If there is damage to the shower valve or it doesn't match any available parts it may be considered for replacement.

Can a leaking shower damage my bathroom flooring?

Yes. Leaking from your shower connections, drain, shower pan, or shower unit can affect the surrounding flooring. Before repairing damaged surfaces, find out what the cause is.

When should I get a shower installed?

If there is significant damage to the shower unit, shower base, or underlying structure, it may be best to install a new shower. However, smaller issues with the shower fixtures and plumbing can be repaired.
