Benefits of Installing a Tankless Water Heater

There are a number of benefits to using a tankless water heater. These will depend on your equipment, installation, usage and energy costs.

Instant Hot Water

As the water is heated as it goes through the system you will not have to wait for the tank to refill.

However, tankless water heaters have limitations. If you have multiple showers, appliances or fixtures running at the same time you may not get the right temperature and flow from the system.

This will depend on the heating capacity and incoming water temperature.

Space Saving

Many tankless water heaters are compact and can be fitted onto a wall. This can help save you space on the floor.

However, we will need to ensure the installation is in line with the manufacturers guidelines. We wouldn’t want to fit a compact system somewhere that won’t allow for appropriate servicing.

Low Standby Loss

As there isn’t a large volume of hot water being stored this can help reduce any standby losses.

However, the energy used will depend on your hot water demand, efficiency of the system, fuel costs, incoming water temperature and quality of the installation. It doesn’t mean that it will be cheaper to run on all properties.

Consider the initial costs of the equipment and installation when considering an upgrade.

Choosing the Right Tankless Water Heater

When choosing the Right Tankless Water Heater you will need to consider the type of fuel it uses, heating capacity and what it will be used for. If you are researching the best tankless water heater installation rockingham nc, the appropriate system will depend on your home’s requirements.

Gas or Electric Tankless Water Heater

Gas tankless water heaters use a burner to heat the water through a heat exchanger. Depending on the model it will run off of either natural gas or propane. We will need to ensure there is an appropriate fuel source, venting, combustion air and plumbing connections. This includes considerations for tankless gas water heater installation and gas tankless water heater installation near me.

We may need to check the gas line to see if there is enough fuel to provide. If it has a higher heating capacity then your current water heater may require more gas.

Electric tankless water heaters use heating elements to heat the water as it passes through. As there is no gas combustion venting required many whole house systems will need a large electrical capacity. This may require electric tankless water heater installation.

We will need to ensure your home has the correct electrical connections, circuit and wiring. We may need to make some changes to the electrical connections.

Depending on your homes energy connections, hot water demand, installation location and operation the right system can be chosen.

Correct Sizing

One of the most important parts of installing a tankless water heater is ensuring it is the right size.

Tankless water heaters are generally looked at using:

  • Flow rate – How much hot water you need when using multiple fixtures.
  • Temperature rise – The difference between the incoming water temperature and the hot water temperature. For example if you frequently have 2 showers and a sink on at the same time you will need a different capacity.

If the system is too small you may find it struggles to provide the correct temperature. If it is too large then it can increase the costs of the equipment and installation without providing much benefit.

We can look at the fixtures you will be using and check the manufacturers guidelines for the heating capacity to choose the right model.

Point of Use or Whole House

Point of use tankless water heaters are designed to provide hot water to a specific fixture or low demand. This can be useful for areas where it is inconvenient to have hot water from a central system.

Whole house tankless water heaters are designed to provide hot water to multiple fixtures. The size you will require will depend on how many fixtures you have, what flow rate they require and how often they will be used.

Depending on your plumbing layout and hot water demand the right system can be chosen. Some installs may require multiple units.

Common Considerations

Even if you choose the Right Tankless Water Heater there may be performance issues if it hasn’t been considered for your home.

Incorrect Sizing

If the system isn’t large enough to provide the correct flow rate and temperature rise then it may struggle when there is high hot water demand. The size should be chosen based on the fixtures that will be used at the same time.

Incorrect Gas or Electricity

Gas and electric systems will have specific energy requirements. If there is not enough gas or electricity then the system may struggle to operate.

This should be checked before choosing the unit.

Incorrect Venting

Gas tankless water heaters require the correct combustion air and venting. This should be done according to the manufacturers guidelines.

Incorrect venting can cause issues with the system and cause safety concerns.

Incorrect Location

The unit should be installed in an area that has the correct space, accessibility and environment. It can also affect how quickly the hot water reaches fixtures further away.

Not Considering Water Quality

Scale can build up inside tankless systems. If your water has dissolved minerals then this can be an issue. Depending on the model and water we may require the correct maintenance or water treatment.

Considering these factors can help prevent unnecessary performance issues.

After Installation Tankless Water Heater Maintenance

It is important to carry out maintenance on your tankless water heater to ensure it is operating as per the specifications. This will depend on the manufacturer, model, water quality and usage.

Descaling

Over time scale can build up in the heat exchanger. Some systems will require descaling/ flushing to help keep the heat transfer and flow.

We will follow the manufacturers guidelines to ensure it is serviced at the correct intervals. We will use the correct cleaning solution and equipment.

Filters

Some tankless water heaters have filters on the incoming water. These can become blocked with debris. We can clean or service the filters according to the manufacturers guidelines to ensure the water flow is correct.

Gas systems may also need the combustion and venting components to be checked.

Monitoring

Watch out for any changes to the hot water supply, temperature, noises and error codes.

If the system is acting differently then we may be able to assess it to see if it needs servicing, repairing or changing.

Having the model information and maintenance information can help a service professional know what to do.

FAQ

Is a tankless water heater suitable for my home?

A tankless water heater may be suitable if the system can provide the hot water for your home and your home has the correct gas or electricity. We can look at how many fixtures will be used at the same time, incoming water temperature and installation requirements to ensure the right size is chosen.

How long will it take to install a tankless water heater?

It will depend on whether it is a simple changeover or a conversion. It may take longer if there are changes to the plumbing, gas line, electricity and venting. We can give you a more accurate time frame after assessing your home.

Can I change my traditional water heater?

Yes you can often change your traditional water heater to a tankless system. However there may be changes required to the plumbing, energy, venting and location. The new unit will need to be the correct size for your hot water demand.

Are gas or electric tankless water heaters better?

It depends on your home. Gas systems will require the correct fuel and venting whereas electric systems will require a sufficient electricity. It will depend on your homes existing connections, hot water demand, equipment and operation.

Will I have unlimited hot water?

A tankless system doesn’t have a stored tank however it will have limitations. It will depend on how much heating capacity and flow it has. If there is more demand than the unit can cope with then the temperature may drop. It is important to ensure it is the correct size.

How much will a tankless water heater installation cost in Rockingham, NC?

The costs will depend on what equipment is being used and how complex the installation is. There may be changes to the plumbing, gas, electricity and venting. If it is a conversion from a storage then it may be different to changing from an existing tankless system. Ask for a detailed breakdown based on your home.

Do I need to regularly service my tankless water heater?

Yes. Depending on the model and water you may need to descale, clean filters and check the relevant components. We recommend following the manufacturers servicing schedule.

Can a tankless water heater provide hot water to multiple bathrooms?

A correctly sized whole house tankless water heater may be able to provide hot water to multiple bathrooms and fixtures. This will depend on how much flow is required at the same time and the temperature rise. It should be sized based on the actual usage requirements of your property.
