Signs You may Need Pipe Repair

Not all pipe issues will be immediately obvious. Sometimes there are signs that will show that there could be an issue with your pipes. Below outlines some of the signs.

Visible Leaking

One of the most obvious signs that there is an issue with your pipes is if you can see water leaking. This could be dripping from your exposed pipe, leaking underneath your sink, or water coming up around your plumbing connections. There may also be leaks within your walls or underneath your flooring.

Common signs of an issue include:

  • Water stains on your ceiling/walls
  • Wet cabinet underneath your sink
  • Peeling paint or bubbling surfaces
  • Wet areas around exposed plumbing
  • Pooling water around connections
  • Constant dampness

Even if there is only a small amount of leaking, constantly having dampness can affect the materials around the area. It could also mean that your pipe or connection is beginning to degrade.

Low Water Pressure

If you notice a sudden drop in pressure then it could be that there is a plumbing issue. This can be caused by damage to your water supply pipe, leaking connection, partial blockage or other issues.

If you are only having low pressure from one fixture then it could just be an issue with that fixture or connection. If multiple fixtures are affected then it could be an issue with a larger section of your plumbing. An assessment can be carried out to see if pipe repair is required.

Strange Noises and Discolouration

You may hear strange noises coming from your pipes. This could be due to pressure changes, loose pipe supports, or an issue with your valve. This does not necessarily mean there is damage to your pipe.

Discolouration can also be a reason to look into. Sometimes rust coloured water can be caused by corrosion within your piping or plumbing. However, there maybe other factors affecting your water supply. If there is constant discolouration then it is important to find out what is causing the issue.

Common Issues with Pipes

There are a number of reasons why plumbing pipes can have issues. Depending on what is affecting your pipes and how much damage there is, we can repair just that section or suggest a larger repair.

Corrosion & Old Pipes

Some pipe materials can corrode over time. This can cause issues with your metal pipes and connections. Over time it can cause holes to appear. Old plumbing can vary greatly depending on what material it was made from, how it was installed and how well it was maintained.

If there is only a small amount of corrosion then we may be able to repair just the section. If there is damage to multiple sections then we may suggest replacing your pipes.

Cracking & Loose Connections

Pipes can crack or leak at connections and fittings. This can be caused by physical damage, incorrect connections, movement or wear.

Any leaking connections will need to be corrected. If there is a crack in the pipe then we may need to replace the section. We can ensure we use the correct method to repair the pipe depending on what material it is. This may include burst pipe repair if the pipe has suddenly broken.

High or Fluctuating Pressure

Having high or fluctuating pressure can cause stress to your pipes, valves and fittings. Over time this can cause leaking and failure.

We would want to look into the pressure issues. If we just repair the leak then the pressure issue will still be present and cause further issues with that section of plumbing or other components.

Not all pipes can be repaired. Depending on how much damage there is we can advise you on the best option.

This means we are only targeting the issue and not replacing the whole line. However we may still want to check the surrounding pipes for any signs of corrosion or damage.

What to Do in a Leak Emergency

If your pipe is leaking or bursting then it can cause fast water damage. Taking the correct actions can help reduce the impact.

If you find there is leaking then:

  • Turn off the nearest accessible water valve if it is safe to do so.
  • Turn off the main water supply if needed.
  • Ensure nobody is near any water that is close to electrical items.
  • Move any furniture or items away from the area if safe.
  • Use towels or containers to control small amounts of water if there is no safety risk.
  • Contact a plumbing professional to assess the damage to your pipe.

Do not touch any electrical items or stand in any water if there is a risk of an electrical hazard. If there is lots of water spreading then focus on keeping yourself safe and get help quickly.

Depending on the company you use there may be emergency repairs available. We would advise you to check what times they are available.

How to Prevent Future Issues

Looking after your plumbing regularly can help spot any issues developing. Whilst it may not stop all pipe failures it can help spot any warning signs.

Try to:

  • Regularly check for any leaking or corrosion on accessible pipes.
  • Check underneath sinks and around plumbing for any dampness.
  • Deal with any regular pressure issues.
  • Replace any visible damaged hoses/connections.
  • Do not put heavy items against exposed pipes.
  • Investigate any constant water stains or mouldy smells.
  • Be aware of any unexpected increase in water usage.
  • Deal with any regular leaking rather than patching.

If your pipe has failed more than once it is important to know why. This could be due to degradation of the material, pressure issues, connection problems or something else.

FAQ

What are common signs of damaged plumbing pipes?

Common signs include visible leaking, water stains, dampness, low water pressure, strange noises and unexpected water usage. Some damage may not be visible so an inspection may be required.

Can leaking pipes be repaired?

Yes, if there is only a small amount of damage and the surrounding pipe is in good condition then we may be able to repair just that section. Depending on the material, condition and location of the pipe we can find the best solution.

How do I know if I need pipe repair in Rockingham, NC?

If you are noticing constant leaking, water damage, regular plumbing issues or unexpected pressure changes then an assessment can be carried out to find out what the issue is. If there is damage to your plumbing then pipe repair would be suitable.

Is drain pipe repair the same as drain cleaning?

No, drain cleaning clears any blockages from your drain. Drain pipe repair deals with any physical damage such as cracks, broken sections or disconnected connections. Depending on the condition of your drain you may need one or both.

Can pipes behind walls or under flooring be repaired?

Yes, often hidden pipes can be repaired. However we may need to access them. How much we need to open and what repair we carry out will depend on where the damaged section is and how the plumbing is set up.

What should I do if my pipe bursts?

If it is safe to do so turn off the main water supply. Stay away from any potential electrical hazards. Try to protect your items and get plumbing help as soon as possible. Do not go near any standing water if there is an electrical item involved.

Will I need to cut into my wall or flooring for pipe repair?

Not always. If the pipe is accessible then we may not need to open up any materials. If it is hidden then we may need to access it. How much we open will depend on where the pipe is and what repair we are carrying out.

How much will pipe repair cost in Rockingham, NC?

The cost will depend on what material the pipe is, where it is, how much damage there is, how much access is required and if we need to repair or replace. We would generally need to assess the plumbing issue to give an accurate price.
