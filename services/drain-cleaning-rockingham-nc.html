Sewer Line Inspection, Repairs, Replacement & Installation

A drain camera inspection in Rockingham NC uses a special camera attached to equipment that can be inserted into accessible areas of your drain/ sewer line. This helps to identify what is happening inside the line.

This can show:

  • Cracks/ damage to the piping.
  • Roots.
  • Build up/ blockage.
  • Disconnected/ misaligned piping.
  • Areas of damaged piping.
  • Blockages that may be causing regular backups.

Camera inspections can help identify where the issue may be however this will depend on how accessible the piping is. It can be useful to have a camera inspection to help decide if cleaning, repairing or replacing is the best option.

Hydro Jetting in Rockingham, NC

Hydro jetting uses specialist equipment to pass high pressured water through your drain/ sewer line. This can help to remove grease, sludge and other build ups from suitable piping.

How It Works

During the process high pressured water will be passed through the drain line at the appropriate access point. The pressure and type of nozzle will depend on what you are trying to clean and the condition of the piping.

Unlike basic methods this can help to clean more of the inside of the piping if possible. It can be useful for certain regular blockages or heavy build ups.

When To Use

Hydro jetting in Rockingham NC may be an option for suitable drain/ sewer lines suffering from grease, sludge or regular build up. However it may not be the best option for every blockage.

Old, delicate, cracked or heavily damaged piping may not be suitable. It is best to have an inspection of the line. For certain situations using a mechanical auger may be a safer option.

Top Tips For Drain Maintenance

Whilst you can't avoid all blockages regular maintenance can help avoid any unnecessary issues.

  • Don't pour cooking greases and oils down your sink, put them in an appropriate container.
  • Use drain strainers to catch any hair or food.
  • Don't flush wipes or paper towels.
  • Clean any accessible drain covers to remove any visible debris.
  • If you notice regular slow drainage pay attention. If multiple fixtures are backing up book an inspection.
  • If you are regularly getting blockages investigate the cause.

FAQs

When Should I Book Emergency Drain Cleaning in Rockingham, NC?

If you are experiencing an overflow of waste water, multiple fixtures backing up or a blockage stopping essential drainage you may want to book an emergency Drain Cleaning Service. Stop using the affected fixtures and avoid any contact with the sewage whilst arranging an inspection.

What Is The Difference Between Drain Cleaning And Clogged Drain Repairs?

Drain cleaning will clear the blockage. Clogged drain repairs may be required if there is an issue with the piping, connection or something else causing the blockage to come back.

Can I Use Hydro Jetting To Clear My Main Sewer Line?

Yes, hydro jetting can be used to clear certain build ups from your sewer line. However if the piping is damaged or delicate it may not be the best option. An inspection may be required.

When Would I Need A Drain Camera Inspection?

A camera inspection can be useful if you are experiencing regular blockages, multiple fixtures affected or suspect an issue with your sewer line. It can identify any visible blockages and structural issues with accessible piping.

Does A Slow Kitchen Or Bathroom Drain Always Mean There Is An Issue With My Sewer Line?

No, it can be due to a localised blockage, branch line issue or more general drainage issue. Knowing where the issue is and what the symptoms are will help identify what needs inspecting.

Should I Repair Or Replace My Damaged Sewer Line?

It depends on how much damage there is and where. If there is a small issue it may be possible to repair. If there is a lot of damage it may be better to replace.

How Can I Avoid Regular Blockages?

Use drain strainers, dispose of greases correctly, don't flush anything inappropriate and address any slow drainage. If you are regularly getting blockages have an investigation to see if there is a deeper blockage or piping issue.
