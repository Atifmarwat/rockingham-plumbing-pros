Sewer Line Inspection Rockingham NC

Problems can occur with your sewer line which aren't obvious to spot. If you're experiencing slow draining, regular blockages, bad smells or damp patches in your garden it could be that there is something wrong with your underground sewer line. By using a professional Sewer Line Inspection Rockingham NC service you can see what the status of your underground sewer pipe is. When Should I Get My Sewer Line Inspected?

You'll have a main sewer line running through your property. This will take all the wastewater from your property to the correct sewer connection/wastewater system. If there is any damage, blockage or misalignment of the pipe then issues can arise with the wastewater flowing. Most of the pipe will be underground so issues may not be obvious until you start seeing symptoms.

Having an inspection can help identify why you're having regular drainage issues. It can also be beneficial if you're looking to purchase a property, do any renovations or just want to know the status of an old sewer system.

Signs You Should Consider Getting an Inspection

If you're experiencing any of the following issues then you should consider getting an inspection:

  • Multiple blockages – If you're experiencing multiple blockages in your drains it could be that there is a blockage or issue in your shared drainage line.
  • Slow Draining – If you're experiencing multiple slow drains in sinks, baths or toilets then it could be a larger issue.
  • Bad Smells – If you're regularly experiencing bad smells from your drains, clean outs or outside then it could be a drainage/sewer issue.
  • Gurgling – If you're getting bubbling from your toilets and drains it could be due to air pockets in the drainage system.
  • Damp or Lush Patches – If there is a damp patch or overly lush area in your garden over your sewer line then you should consider an inspection.
  • Regular Plumbing Issues – If you're regularly experiencing blockages then it could be due to an issue with the pipes.

Although these symptoms don't guarantee that there is damage to your sewer line, having a professional inspection can help identify if the issue is with your internal drainage system, main sewer line or elsewhere.

Why Multiple Drain Issues Could Mean an Issue with Your Main Sewer Line

If you're experiencing blockages in one sink or shower then this will usually only affect that specific item. However, if you're experiencing issues with multiple items then it could be that there is an issue with your main drain/sewer line.

For example, if your toilet is bubbling when using the shower nearby then it could be due to a blockage causing the wastewater to not flow freely. If you're experiencing backflow into a lower toilet then it could be that there is a blockage further along your drainage system.

Having an inspection can help identify where the issue is.

What is a Sewer Line Inspection?

A sewer line inspection is when we inspect the underground pipe which takes the wastewater away from your property. This can be done with a specialist waterproof camera which we insert into your accessible sewer clean out. A Residential sewer line inspection rockingham nc can help property owners understand the condition of their underground sewer pipes.

The camera will show us what's going on inside the pipe using a monitor. This can help us identify any obvious blockages, damage and other issues.

A sewer camera inspection can give us more insight into the condition of your pipe compared to a simple drain inspection. This will help us decide if any further work needs to be done.

How Do We Carry Out a Sewer Camera Inspection?

We will generally do the following:

  • Identify Access – Identify where a clean out or access point can be found.
  • Prepare Camera – Attach our flexible camera onto the cable.
  • Insert Camera – Insert the camera into the accessible parts of your sewer pipe.
  • Inspect Pipe – Look for any blockages, cracks, root ingress, water build up and other issues.
  • Note Key Issues – Note any key issues.
  • Discuss Options – Decide if you need to clean, repair, further inspect or replace based on what we have found.

How far we can go will depend on the condition of the pipe, how accessible it is, bends, blockages and equipment. We may not be able to get through a heavily blocked/collapsed section.

What Can a Sewer Camera Show Us?

A properly carried out sewer camera inspection can help us identify:

  • Root Ingress – If there is a small gap, damaged joint or crack in your pipe then tree roots can enter.
  • Cracks – If there are any cracks, breaks or obvious damage to the pipe.
  • Blockages – If there is any build up of grease or blockages.
  • Foreign Objects – If there are any foreign objects blocking the wastewater.

The images from the camera can be very helpful however won't always show us everything underground. If there is a leak on the outside of the pipe we may need to do some further locating to identify exactly where it is.

Common Issues Identified

There can be many issues with underground sewer pipes depending on how old they are, what they're made from, how they were installed, surrounding conditions and how well they've been maintained. It is important to know what the actual issue is as different issues will require different solutions.

Root Ingress

Tree roots will naturally grow towards moisture. If there is a small gap, damaged joint or crack in your pipe then tree roots can enter.

As the roots grow they can cause blockages by catching toilet paper and other waste. Having a camera inspection can help us identify any obvious root ingress.

Removing the roots can help temporarily however it may not fix the issue with the gap. Depending on the condition of the pipe further repairs may be needed.

Cracked / Broken / Collapsed Pipes

The pipe can become cracked or broken due to deterioration, ground movement, outside pressure or other issues. If there is a large amount of damage then it could be causing issues with the wastewater flowing and leaking into the surrounding soil.

We may be able to identify any obvious cracks, missing sections of pipe or collapsed section using the camera. Depending on how bad the damage is and where it is we can decide the best course of action. If it is a localised issue then we can repair it. If there is a lot of damage then we may need to replace it. If you are searching for sewer pipe repair near me, an inspection can help identify whether the pipe has damage that may need repairing.

Blockages

Grease, wipes, paper products and other items can build up in the sewer line. Even items which claim to be flushable may cause blockages as they may not break down like toilet paper.

Having an inspection can help us identify if there is a blockage and where. If we can't get the camera through then we may need to clean it.

Misaligned / Sagging Pipes

The sewer pipes need to be correctly aligned and sloped to take the wastewater to the connection. If the pipes are becoming misaligned or sagging then there may be a low section causing water and waste to build up.

This is sometimes known as a belly. We may be able to identify water build up and misalignment using the camera however further assessment may be needed to identify the cause and best solution.

Domestic and Commercial

Both domestic and commercial properties will need their wastewater drainage system working correctly. However the layout and requirements can vary.

Domestic inspections may be for a single family home, multi story or shared drainage. Common reasons for wanting an inspection would be regular toilet blockages, multiple slow drains, sewer smells and old underground pipes.

Commercial inspections can be for larger drainage, multiple items and higher volumes of wastewater. Restaurants, offices, retail buildings and others can suffer from issues such as grease, high usage, build up and pipe damage.

We will consider the layout of the plumbing and how accessible the sewer line is. Larger or more complex drainage may need more planning.

Sewer Inspection vs Cleaning

A sewer inspection and cleaning are two different things.

A sewer line inspection will help us understand the condition of the inside of the pipe. We can use a camera to identify any obvious blockages, root ingress, cracks, separated joints and other issues.

A sewer line cleaning will help us remove any build up or blockages. Depending on the blockage and condition of the pipe we may use mechanical equipment or another suitable method. If you are searching for sewer cleaning near me, it is important to understand that cleaning and inspection are two different services.

Cleaning won't necessarily fix any structural issues and an inspection won't necessarily remove any blockage.

If you're experiencing regular sewer issues it's important to know the difference. Using a camera can help us decide if it's likely the issue can be resolved with cleaning or if there is damage to the pipe.
