What Is Hydro Jetting?

Hydro jetting uses specialised equipment to insert a high-pressure hose and nozzle to clean your drains/ sewers. This uses water to help bust up any deposits and clean them away.

Whilst this is often used for regular blockage, and grease deposits, it isn't suitable for all pipes. You should consider the state of your pipes to see if it will help.

How High Pressure Water Cleans Drains

Hydro jetting normally involves a water source, high-pressure pump, special hose and a nozzle. This nozzle will have holes in it that will push the water forwards and backwards to help clean your pipes. The front jets can bust up any blockages, whilst the back jets help the hose move through the pipe and clear any loose debris.

A professional will use this equipment and choose a suitable pressure and nozzle. This needs to be a suitable pressure for the state of your pipes and deposits. Too much pressure could damage weakened or deteriorated pipes.

What Equipment Is Used?

Professional equipment can include:

  • High pressure water pump – To create the required pressure.
  • Jetting Hose – To send the high pressure water into your drains/sewers.
  • Special Nozzles – To create different patterns to suit your blockage and piping.
  • Water Source – To provide the water for your drains.
  • Camera – A sewer camera can be used pre/post cleaning if possible.

The equipment used will depend on the size of your pipes, blockage and drainage system.

Will Hydro Jetting Damage My Pipes?

No, not all plumbing will be suitable for hydro jetting. This will depend on the material, age, condition and pressure your pipes can handle.

Old, fragile, corroded, cracked or damaged pipes can become damaged further if we use the wrong pressure. If there is a serious blockage we may need to carry out an inspection or a different clearing method.

Hydro Jetting vs Drain Snaking

Hydro jetting and drain snaking are two different methods.

A drain snake uses a flexible cable/ auger to reach and bust up blockages. This can help with localised blockages.

Hydro jetting uses high pressure water to bust up and clear suitable deposits. This can be a good option if you have grease, sludge or regular deposits.

Depending on your blockage and pipe condition:

  • Drain Snaking – Often a good option for localised blockages and certain solid blockages.
  • Hydro Jetting – May be suitable if you have grease, sludge and deposits. This can be a good option for suitable sections of pipe.
  • Camera – Can help investigate the inside of your pipes if there are concerns about damage or regular issues.

Neither will fix damage to your pipes. A professional can help decide which is the right option for you.
