Drain Cleaning Services in Rockingham, NC

Drains help remove wastewater from your sink, shower, bath, toilet and other plumbing fittings. Over time this can cause a build up of grease, soap scum, hair, food and other substances causing your drainpipes to become blocked.

We can professionally clean your drains to remove any blockages.

Common Signs of Blocked Drains

If your drain is partially blocked you may notice slow draining from your sink or bath. There may also be bubbling noises, frequent blockages and unpleasant smells.

If multiple fittings are draining slowly there may be a larger blockage within your main branch drain.

Professional Drain Cleaning

Depending on where the blockage is and how bad it is, we can use specialist drain cleaning equipment to remove any build up.

Hydro jetting can be used if the blockage is suitable for this. This uses high pressure water to clean out the inside of your drainpipe. However, this should be considered depending on the condition of your pipes. Older or damaged pipes may need a different service.

If you are having frequent blockages you should investigate where the issue is coming from.

Sewer Line Services

Your sewer line takes wastewater away from your property. If there is an issue with this it can cause problems with multiple plumbing fittings. We can help with this.

Common Sewer Line Issues

Issues with your sewer line can occur due to build up, roots, degradation, ground movement and damage. You may notice:

  • Multiple drains backing up.
  • Persistent sewage smells.
  • Gurgling from toilets and drains.
  • Wastewater around floor drains.
  • Frequent blockages.

Whilst these can be signs of a sewer line issue they may not always be. Getting a professional to investigate can help determine if it is a main line issue.

Sewer Line Inspection, Repairs & Replacements

Using a sewer camera can help us find any cracks, roots, blockages or other issues within accessible pipework. If replacement is required this depends on the type of pipework, damage and location.

Leak Detection & Repairs

There can be leaks within visible plumbing fittings or hidden behind walls, under flooring and around underground water pipework. Even small leaks can cause wastage and moisture damage.

Signs of Hidden Leaks

Not all leaks will cause a pool of water but you may notice increased water usage, damp areas, discolouration, mouldy smells and running water noises.

You can investigate by looking at accessible fittings and keeping an eye on your water meter. However, if the leak is hidden you may need a professional to use specialist equipment to find it.

Leak Detection & Repairs

Depending on where you think the leak is we can investigate visible fittings, test sections of your plumbing or use specialist equipment to pinpoint the issue.

Once found we can tighten or replace any fittings, repair any section of pipe or replace any damaged fittings. This will depend on the type of pipework, accessibility and extent of damage.

Pipe Repairs & Replacements

Pipes provide water to your fittings and remove wastewater from your building. Issues can occur due to corrosion, damage, loose fittings, freezing and degradation.

Common Pipe Issues

You may experience leaking joints, cracked pipes, corrosion, low water pressure or frequent pipe failures. Depending on where the pipes are and what type they are we can help with the right repairs.

If you have a visible leak around your joint we may only need to carry out a small amount of repairs. If there is extensive corrosion or frequent failures we may need to look at a larger section.

Repair or Replace?

If there is only a small amount of damage and the rest of the pipework is in good condition we may be able to repair. However, if there is extensive corrosion, multiple leaks or structural damage we may recommend replacement.

A professional can help you compare the condition of your pipework to how reliable new pipework would be.

What to Expect When a Professional Plumber Visits

Generally, your plumber will start by asking about your issue. Telling them when the issue started, what fittings are affected and if you have noticed any changes in water pressure or drainage will help them investigate.

They may then:

  • Investigate the affected plumbing fittings and accessible pipework.
  • Investigate possible causes.
  • Explain what repairs or replacements can be carried out.
  • Discuss what they will do and any costs involved.
  • Carry out the agreed service.
  • Test the affected plumbing fittings.

If you have a complicated issue such as underground leaks, sewer line damage or large pipe replacements this may differ.

Cost of Plumbing Services

The cost of plumbing work can vary depending on what the issue is and how much work is required. Simple repairs to your fittings will be different to replacing underground water lines or large sewer line work.

Factors that can affect the cost include:

  • Type of service – Drain cleaning, leak detection, fitting repairs and pipe replacements will all be different.
  • Severity of issue – If there is lots of damage or frequent blockages more work may be required.
  • Accessibility – Pipes behind walls, under flooring or underground can be more difficult to access.
  • Replacement of pipes, fittings, valves and equipment.
  • Labour – How long and complex the work is.
  • Other repairs – If there are other water or plumbing issues they may need to be addressed.

Ask for an explanation of the recommended repairs and what they will cost you.

FAQs

How can I find a reliable plumber in Rockingham, NC?

Look for a plumber who can explain the issue, clearly explain what they will do and confirm they are licensed and insured. Ask if they have experience with the service you need and ask for a quote. If you are searching for a licensed plumber near me, you should also confirm the plumber's licensing and insurance.

When should I call a plumber for blocked drains?

If your drain isn't clearing with basic maintenance, is frequently blocking or multiple fittings are affected you may want to get them professionally cleaned. Avoid using strong chemical cleaners frequently if you aren't sure what is causing the blockage.

How do I know if I have a sewer line issue?

If you have multiple slow drains, frequent backups, sewage smells and gurgling it may be an issue with your sewer line. This can have other causes so an investigation may be required.

Should I repair or replace my pipes?

This will depend on the condition and extent of damage. If there is a small leak and the rest of your pipes are in good condition we may be able to repair. However, if there is lots of corrosion or frequent failures it may be more practical to replace.

What should I do if I find a water leak?

If it is safe to do so turn off the relevant water supply or main water valve to your property to reduce any further water damage. Avoid walking in any water near electrical equipment and arrange for the correct investigation.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

This will depend on the condition, age, type of failure and repairs required. A professional can help determine if it makes more sense to repair your current unit or replace.

How regularly should I have my plumbing checked?

This will depend on how old your property is, type of pipework, how much it's used and any previous issues. Having regular checks on visible pipework, fittings, drains and water using equipment can help identify any issues.
